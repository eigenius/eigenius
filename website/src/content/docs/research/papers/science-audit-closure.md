## What the paper will cover

A scientific-method contribution framing the reproducibility crisis
as in substantial part a *crisis of the collapsed audit chain*.
The paper will describe how a typed, content-addressed knowledge
graph closes the gap for AI-mediated scientific output, with
structural alignment to the FAIR and ALCOA+ data-integrity
frameworks. The drug-screening worked example will appear in the
supplement as ESL specimens of the domain-bridge declaration and
the reasoning certificate.

## In the meantime

The conceptual material the paper will cover is available now in
narrative form on this site:

- The [**Drug-screening example**](/examples/drug-screening/) is
