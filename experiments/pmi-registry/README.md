# UAB Round 1, experiment 02 — a PMI registry case on the chain

The package (`UAB Round 1/02-synthetic-pmi-registry/`) holds three synthetic cases in the shape of
the PMI Case Registry's Airtable export, the registry schema, and an analyst's working notes for
SYN-26-002. It asks "is this the kind of case Eigenius can work, and what would you need in hand to
start?" (`README.md:11-12`). Its check is the notes' decomposition of SYN-26-002: seven claims,
each assigned Observed, Declared, Ill-typed or "Declared bridge" (`SYN-26-002-analyst-notes.md:78-86`),
and four items the analyst could not type (`:117-128`). The design behind this folder is
`docs/design/d99-uab-registry-case-typing.md` (D99).

This folder is the whole experiment as ESL, one file per layer, numbered in load order. The kernel
validates each layer when it is committed, so every statement in files 01–07 type-checks, and every
certificate in 07 is checked against the grounds it cites. File 08 holds what the kernel refuses.

**Terms.** A *declaration* is a proposition with the agent who asserts it and the source they rest
on; its trace is what lets other resources cite it (a *witness*). A citation names its ground:
*Declared* (somebody stands behind it), *Observed* (an instrument or dataset produced it, by an
observation trace), or *Verified* (a proof of it was checked). A *conclusion* is a certificate:
grounds, combined by application and instantiation, that the kernel checks against the
proposition they are claimed to ground.

## What the kernel refused, and what it shows

| Refused | File | The kernel's reason | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Responsive(p.Leu44Pro)`, citing the survey | 08 | `no admitted IsDeclaredAs witness for IRI …:claim_6` | The nephrologist's inference. The survey says some responsive allele is at Leu44 and names none, so it grounds nothing about p.Leu44Pro |
| `Responsive(c.131T>C)`, citing the survey | 08 | `… (is_a = [variant:NucleotideAllele]) does not inhabit class variant:ProteinAllele` | At the resolution the proband carries, the inference is not statable: responsiveness is a property of the protein allele, and a protein-level claim reaches a nucleotide allele only through `TranslatesTo` and `ProteinMediated` |
| "The chaperone agents reduce his urine output", citing the hypothesis | 08 | `no admitted IsDeclaredAs witness for IRI …:chaperone_hypothesis` | A hypothesis is recorded, can answer the family's third question, and grounds nothing: nobody declares the step from cell models to the child |
| Each of the 15 claims, cited as Observed | (test) | no admitted `IsObservedAs` witness | Nothing in the package is Observed: no tier-3 record is held. The supply table below says what would change that |

The converter, not the kernel, held back SYN-26-002's `HP:0004918` row. HP:0004918 is
"Hyperchloremic metabolic acidosis", and the row's label, "Hypernatremic dehydration", is
HP:0004906's in all 18 releases checked. The other findings are in `04-registry-report.md`.

The kernel's own tests for the vocabularies refuse four further substitutions
(`kernel/tests/d99_layers.rs`, `kernel/tests/disequality_by_field.rs`):
- `Carries` of a protein allele;
- `TranslatesTo` from a protein allele to a nucleotide allele;
- `At` at the wrong position (`LitInt(45) ≠ LitInt(44)`);
- distinctness by name, or of two alleles that agree on every field.

## Choices made in building the chain

D99 does not settle these; each is open to revision.

- `ProteinMediated(c.131T>C)` is declared by the analyst. D99 8c lists it under the registry, but the registry has no field for it, and the notes' mechanism argument assumes it.
- `Responsive` is defined over `syngene:survey_cutpoint`, a constant whose value the package does not state, traced to the survey's authors. Claim 5's "somebody chose the cut-point" is that trace.
- The mechanism bridge is declared at 4%, the value the case needs. The notes state no threshold.
- The mother's first question is read as "is every allele the survey's cohort carried c.131T>C".
- A declaration whose own date is not in the package carries the date its source reached PMI (medical records received 2026-06-04, analyst work-up 2026-07-15), and says so in `prov:rationale`.
- The registry's ClinVar statements name one release, unrecorded.
- Genes are minted from the registry's symbol (`urn:eigenius:pmi:gene:<symbol>`), not resolved to `ncbi:Gene` (D99 §9). Alleles are `urn:eigenius:variant:allele:<accession>_<interval>_<alt>` (D99 §4), so `05-syngene.esl` mints c.130C>T and p.Leu44Phe under the converter's scheme.

## Reproducing

```bash
# 04: the registry layer and report. Needs the UAB package (experiments/uab, gitignored),
# references/hpo/hp.json, and the HPO history (scripts/provision-hpo-history.sh).
cargo run -p eigenius-pmi-registry --bin pmi-registry-convert -- \
  --cases "experiments/uab/UAB Round 1/02-synthetic-pmi-registry/synthetic-cases.json" \
  --out experiments/pmi-registry/04-registry.esl \
  --report experiments/pmi-registry/04-registry-report.md

# 01–08 on the bootstrap chain, WordNet and HPO stubbed; the converter on its own fixture
cargo test -p eigenius-pmi-registry

# 04 is the converter's output on the package (needs the package and references/hpo)
cargo test -p eigenius-pmi-registry --test emitted_layer_validates -- --ignored

# 01–08 on the lexicon snapshot
EIGENIUS_DB_SNAPSHOT=../db-snapshot/uab-d99-r2-hpo-aligned-2026-10-02 \
  cargo test --release -p eigenius-pmi-registry --test syn_26_002_on_snapshot -- --ignored --nocapture
```

| Test | Asserts |
|---|---|
| `tests/syn_26_002_chain.rs`, `tests/syn_26_002_on_snapshot.rs` | 01–07 validate; 08's three refusals, each with its reason; each claim commits cited by its ground and is refused cited as Observed |
| `tests/emitted_layer_validates.rs` | the converter's rules on a fixture of its own (consent, label check, HGVS check, protein-only rows, compound heterozygosity); the ignored check that 04 is current |
